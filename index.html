Supervised Industrial TrainingSIT Process Flow

Technological University of the Philippines · College of Science · Computer Studies Department

    Created by: Ino Amador

    Phase 1 of 3

    Pre-Deployment

    Eight steps, from handing in your documents to signing the MOA. Do them in order.

    1. 1
      Step 1

      Document Submission & Checklist

      Accomplish and submit the following documents in two (2) long brown envelopes.

      Original Envelope

      • Originally signed Application Form
      • Originally signed Waiver Form
      • Copy of Govt ID (Parent) w/ 3 signatures
      • Original Medical Clearance Slip
      • Original NBI Clearance

      Duplicate Envelope

      • Originally signed Application Form
      • Originally signed Waiver Form
      • Copy of Govt ID (Parent) w/ 3 signatures
      • Photocopy of Medical Clearance Slip
      • Photocopy of NBI Clearance
      Application Form Waiver Form
    2. 2
      Step 2

      Verification & Return

      Once signed by your SIT Coordinator, Department Head, and Director of the IRJP:

      • Original Envelope: Returned to the student.
      • Duplicate Envelope: Kept by the coordinator.
    3. 3
      Step 3

      HTE Selection & Validation

      Select a Host Training Establishment (HTE) through one of two methods:

      Option A: Partner HTE

      Choose from companies with an existing MOA.

      Option B: Personal Choice

      Apply elsewhere. Coordinator must validate legitimacy first.

    4. 4
      Step 4

      Application Process

      If a suitable company is found:

      1. Request Recommendation

      Request Form (GForm)

      2. Submit to Company

      • Signed Recommendation Letter
      • Curriculum Vitae (CV) / Resume
      • Letter of Intent
    5. 5
      Step 5

      Company Acceptance

      Upon acceptance, secure the following:

      • Acceptance Letter
      • Training Plan (Company or Univ Format)
      Training Plan Template
    6. 6
      Step 6

      University Endorsement

      • Fill out Request for Endorsement.
      • Coordinator issues signed Endorsement Letter.
      • Submit a copy of received letter back to Coordinator.
      Endorsement Form (GForm)
    7. 7
      Step 7

      Acceptance Monitoring

      Fill out this form strictly for tracking purposes once accepted.

      Monitoring Form
    8. 8
      Step 8

      MOA & Training Agreement

      Required for new partners, companies without MOA, or expired MOAs

      • Print 5 copies on Long/Legal Bond Paper.
      • Submit to Coordinator for Univ Signing.
      • Student/Coordinator facilitates Company Signing & Notarization.
      MOA Template Training Agreement Template

    Phase 2 of 3

    During Training

    What to keep up with while you render your hours at the company.

    1. 1
      Step 1

      Training Hours Requirement

      BSCS, BSIS, and BSIT students must render a total of 486 hours.

    2. 2
      Step 2

      Daily Work Activity

      Accomplish form daily. Taking pictures of tasks is encouraged if allowed.

      Daily Form
    3. 3
      Step 3

      Overtime (OT) Guidelines

      Must file and sign a request using the form before actual OT rendering.

      OT Form
    4. 4
      Step 4

      Evaluation Grade Request

      Request from HTE upon rendering 75% (364.5 hours) to submit to coordinator.

      Trainee Evaluation Form
    5. 5
      Step 5

      Certificate of Completion

      The student/trainee shall request a Certificate of Completion signed by the supervisor or authorized signatories upon or after rendering the total required 486 hours.

    Phase 3 of 3

    Post-Training

    What to hand in when you finish your hours.

    Final Output Submission

    Compile all final documents in a Long/Legal Yellow Clear Book.

    • Narrative Report
    • Copy of DTR / Attendance
    • Pictures during training
    • Trainee Evaluation (w/ rating)
    • Daily Work Activities
    • HTE Evaluation (w/ rating)
    • Certificate of Completion
